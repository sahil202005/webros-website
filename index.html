Frequently asked questions

Good to know before we start.

Which package should I choose?

Standard is suited to businesses that need a polished informational website. Pro is for businesses that also want an admin panel to manage supported products, services and images.

Is the domain and hosting included?

Domain registration and hosting costs are separate unless specifically included in your written proposal. We'll explain the options before you commit.

Can I request changes after launch?

Six months of maintenance and support are included as described in your package. After that, standard updates are ₹500 each or you can ask about the ₹1,999/year maintenance and updates package. New features may require a separate quote.

Can you build a website for my type of business?

We focus on small businesses such as restaurants, cafés, hotels, salons, gyms, shops, coaching classes and local service providers. Tell us what you do and we'll suggest a suitable scope.

How do I get started?

Send us your business name, business type and the features you need using the contact form. It will prepare a WhatsApp message for you to review and send.
